## Maintained boundary

`zoverions-crg` should retain causal-emergence and coarse-graining numerical work. Cosmological analyses remain exploratory until survey selection, null models, graph construction, mass cuts, sample size, sensitivity, and independent data checks are complete.

Governance material should stop growing inside the numerical package. It is prepared only as a source-side export for a future owner-approved repository.

## Claim boundary

- Effective Information and causal-beta computations require software fixtures and numerical-reference checks.
- A pattern in one graph or survey is not cosmological generalization.
- The constitutional evaluator is a decision-support heuristic, not an objective measure of morality, legitimacy, safety, or constitutional correctness.
- Thermodynamic or complexity language does not itself validate governance outcomes.
